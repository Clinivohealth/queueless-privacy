
# QueueLess Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

## Privacy Policy

QueueLess ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy.

### Information We Collect
- Business name and profile information provided by you.
- Queue and ticket information created within the app.
- QR codes generated for departments.
- Basic account information required for authentication.

### How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Generate and manage digital queues.
- Generate QR codes for customer check-in.
- Improve the reliability and security of the app.

### Data Storage
Your data is securely stored using trusted cloud infrastructure. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

### Third-Party Services
QueueLess may use trusted third-party services such as:
- Supabase (Authentication and Database)
- Google Play Billing (Subscriptions)
- Firebase services (if enabled)

These services have their own privacy policies.

### Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

### Children's Privacy
QueueLess is not intended for children under the age of 13.

### Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
